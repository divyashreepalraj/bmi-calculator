What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple statistical screening ratio of weight to height squared (kg/m²). While widely used by clinicians as an initial assessment, it does not directly measure body fat percentage, skeletal muscle mass, bone density, or fat distribution. Athletes, elderly adults, and pregnant individuals may fall outside standard benchmarks without increased health risks.
